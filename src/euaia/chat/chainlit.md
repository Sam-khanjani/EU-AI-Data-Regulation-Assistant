# EU AI Act Assistant

Ask anything about the EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

**How answers are checked.** Answers are written only from the official text on EUR-Lex.
Every statement has to carry a quote, and each quote is compared word for word with the
provision it cites. A statement whose quote does not match is removed before you see it. If
the regulation does not answer your question, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

**Reading an answer.** The small links after each sentence (`Art. 5`, `Annex III`) are the
provisions that support it. Hover over one to see the quoted text, or click it to open the
provision on EUR-Lex. Open *Checked the EU AI Act* above an answer to see how it was
reached: what was searched, how many quotes were verified, and the text version used.

**Follow-up questions work.** "What about deployers?" is read in the light of the
conversation so far.

**For applicability questions** ("Is my system high-risk?") you get the test the Regulation
applies, criterion by criterion, never a verdict, because that depends on facts only you have.

This is an information tool, not legal advice.
